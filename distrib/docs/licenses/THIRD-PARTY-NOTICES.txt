L4ZY - third-party components and licences
==============================================

L4ZY is a modified client of Cube 2: Sauerbraten (2020 edition), with
ray-traced lighting, NVIDIA DLSS/DLAA, NVIDIA NRD denoising, HDR output,
optional 3D sound (Steam Audio) and a local chat translation service. This
is not an official Sauerbraten release.
