COLOURS. A setting marked "(colour, hex 0xRRGGBB)" takes a hex code: two hex digits (00 to FF) for red, then green, then blue; after 9 come A B C D E F (A=10, F=15), so letters are normal. Write it with 0x in a CMD line: CMD: crosshaircolour 0xFF69B4 (to the player you may write #FF69B4). Common colours: pink FF69B4 (light pink FFB6C1, deep pink FF1493), red FF0000, orange FF8000, yellow FFFF00, green 00FF00 (dark green 008000), cyan 00FFFF, light blue 80C0FF, blue 0000FF, purple 8000FF, magenta FF00FF, white FFFFFF, grey 808080, black 000000, brown 8B4513. Player colours (my colour, friend and team colours) are not hex: they take a list number (-1 none, 0 green, 1 blue, 2 yellow, 3 red, 4 grey, 5 magenta, 6 orange, 7 white, 8 cyan, 9 pink).

WEAPON TRAILS. Each weapon (shotgun, chaingun, rifle, rocket launcher, grenade launcher, pistol) has a trail colour for All (everyone), Me (my own shots; -1 = same as All) and Others (everyone else, any team; -1 = same as All). "My trail" means the Me colours; with no weapon named, it means the six Me colours, one CMD line per weapon. Rockets and grenades also have a projectile and explosion colour (All / Me / Others); with ray tracing their light takes that colour. Trail brightness (25 to 100 %) dims the custom colours. It is a local look: other players never see your colours.

@ rt raytracing ray tracing traced rtx lumiere lumieres light lights lighting eclairage classique classic hwrt pathtracing reflet reflets reflection reflections
RAY TRACING. Lighting "Ray tracing" (Options > Graphics, General tab) replaces the classic lighting with hardware ray-traced light: lamps and glowing lights with real shadows, the sun and its shadow, soft light from the sky, lit player models, sharp reflections on water and glossy surfaces, and weapon lights (muzzle flashes, rockets, explosions). It is not full path tracing. It needs a graphics card with hardware ray tracing (tested on NVIDIA RTX; AMD and Intel not tested); otherwise Ray tracing is greyed out with the reason. "Classic (no RT)" is the original Sauerbraten lighting (the default): fastest, any graphics card. The Ray tracing tab holds shadows, lights and surfaces; the Performance tab holds the profiles.

@ dlss dlaa fsr aa antialiasing anticrenelage crenelage aliasing upscaling flou floue blurry blur net nette nettete sharp sharper sharpness transformer cnn native natif amd nvidia upscale
ANTI-ALIASING AND UPSCALING (Options > Graphics, General tab, Anti-Aliasing). Native (default): no upscaling. DLAA (Deep Learning Anti-Aliasing): NVIDIA's AI anti-aliasing at full resolution, the best picture, a bit slower. DLSS (Deep Learning Super Sampling) Quality / Balanced / Performance: NVIDIA renders fewer pixels and rebuilds the picture with AI: more fps, softer as you go down (Performance is the softest). DLAA and DLSS need an NVIDIA RTX card. FSR Native / Quality / Balanced / Performance: AMD FidelityFX Super Resolution, no AI, any graphics card; FSR sharpening (default 10) adds crispness. Blurry picture with DLSS: use DLSS Quality or DLAA, keep the DLSS model on Transformer (sharper; CNN is softer and slightly faster, used by the High, Medium and Low profiles), raise DLAA/DLSS sharpening (0 by default, 20 is crisper) and tick Sharper textures with DLSS/FSR. FXAA only applies in Native (softer). MSAA (Off / 2x / 4x / 8x, the anti-aliasing of the original Sauerbraten): smooth edges with sharp textures, Classic lighting with Native only; a change restarts the graphics. No frame generation.

DENOISERS. Ray tracing traces few rays per pixel, so the raw light is grainy; a denoiser smooths it. NRD (NVIDIA Real-time Denoisers, an open-source library) cleans the sky light (Sky denoiser: NRD, the default; the older built-in filter always traces 4 rays, slower and grainier in motion). SIGMA is NRD's shadow denoiser: here it smooths the soft shadows of the sun and lamps (Soft shadow denoiser, on by default; it only works while Soft shadows is on, and needs NRD). Grainy soft shadows: keep the soft shadow denoiser on and use 2 or 4 soft shadow rays. Grainy sky shade: more sky rays per pixel. Shadows smearing when you move: lower the denoiser steadiness.

SHADOWS WITH RAY TRACING (Options > Graphics, Ray tracing tab). Soft shadows (On by default; the Low profile turns them off): the edge blurs with the distance from the object and stays sharp where it touches, like real shadows; it costs about 1 to 3 ms; off, shadows are hard (sharp). With soft shadows on: Soft shadow rays (1, 2 = default, 4, 8: more is truer and less grainy, but slower) and the Soft shadow denoiser (NRD SIGMA, on). My own shadow in first person: the sun only by default. Shadows of the others: sun, sky and glowing lights by default. Classic lighting has its own Classic shadows and blob shadows.

RT PROFILES (Options > Graphics, Performance tab; ray tracing only). Ultra (default): best picture. High: same look, about 10-20 % more fps (DLSS model CNN, solid leaves for sky light, lighter NRD, still models from depth). Medium: High + sky light on half the pixels, lightest NRD, sky reach 1024, adaptive rays, 3 lamps with a shadow, 4 glowing lights, 1 soft shadow ray. Low: Medium + RT quality "Half sky, 1 glow", 2 lamps, 2 glowing lights, no soft shadows: darker, fastest. Changing one row yourself shows Custom. To switch profile, propose the profile alone (GFX_profapply 0 to 3): it sets every row itself. Measured on an RTX 4070 Ti SUPER at 1440p with DLAA: over Ultra, High about +6-19 % fps, Medium +17-35 %, Low +35-60 %. Modest graphics card: Medium or Low plus DLSS (NVIDIA RTX) or FSR; without hardware ray tracing, Classic lighting.

@ fps performance performances perf lent lente lag lags saccade saccades fluide fluidite rame ralenti ralentit framerate vitesse rapide faster slow stutter
MORE FPS (biggest gains first). With ray tracing: an RT profile (High, then Medium or Low, Performance tab), then upscaling: DLSS Quality / Balanced / Performance (NVIDIA RTX) or FSR Quality / Balanced / Performance (any card) in General > Anti-Aliasing. Soft shadows cost about 1 to 3 ms: turning them off gives a little more. Classic lighting (no ray tracing) is the fastest of all. Show the frame rate: Show FPS (Options > HUD). V-Sync adds a little delay; Low latency mode (on by default) keeps the mouse quick at no fps cost.

@ hdr sdr nits luminosite brightness monitor moniteur peak pic calibration calibrer
HDR (Options > Graphics, HDR screen tab). SDR is the default. Native HDR sends a real HDR picture to an HDR screen (NVIDIA RTX). Windows HDR must already be on: the game never changes Windows. Then calibrate: exposure, reference white (default 80 nits), peak (automatic from the screen, or manual) and HUD brightness. In native HDR, Creases (SSAO) and motion blur are not applied. If Windows HDR was turned on after the game started, choose Native HDR again.

@ traduction traduire traduis traduit traduite translate translation translated translator chat langue langues language languages tsay trep modele model qwen
CHAT TRANSLATION (Chat Translation page, main menu and Options). A local AI (Qwen3, Small 4B or Normal 8B, installed from the Model section) translates incoming chat into your language: translation in green, original in grey. Nothing leaves your PC unless you enter an internet API key yourself. Settings: translate public / team chat, the language you read and the one you send in, Auto-translate of what you send, preview while typing, "I am a woman" (feminine grammar). Commands: /tsay (say translated), /tsayteam, /trep (reply in the last speaker's language), /tsayr (careful, slower translation). F7 mutes or unmutes translation, F9 retranslates the last line; the Keys page (Chat tab) can put "Say translated" and the others on keys.

RECORDING (Options > Recording). Match videos: matches recorded as videos (on/off, quality, how many to keep). Demos with HD aim: demos that keep your exact aim and play it back exactly; an option also stores which enemies were really on your screen. While watching a demo: Demo back / ahead 10 s and Demo HD aim keys (Keys page, Game and demos tab). Match record and Demo record keys start and stop recording.

KEYS (Options > Keys, tabs Play, Chat, Game and demos, Spectate and edit). The assistant can put on a key any "(key action)" of the list, up to 4 in a row; an action that lasts while the key is held (scoreboard, zoom, attack) goes on the key the same way: CMD: bind Q togglezoom. Third person view only works offline and in edit mode. Pause works in local games and demos (or as master). Spectate on / off makes you spectate, and join again on the next press. Spectator keys only work while spectating, edit keys only in edit mode.

HUD. Every HUD part can be moved with the drag-and-drop HUD editor (Options > HUD, Move HUD parts): drag to move, drag an edge to resize, right click puts a part back, Esc when done; "Put everything back in place" resets all. The ammo bar, game clock, score, kill feed and kill streak also have an Adjust page (size, position, alignment).

SEEING PLAYERS. Fullbright player models (Graphics) lights every player whatever the light of the map: Subtle, Bright, Overbright or Max. Team colours (Friends and colours, Player colours) can paint every enemy in one bright colour (for example magenta or green) and every teammate in another, bots included. Gamma (Display) brightens the whole picture. With ray tracing, dark corners stay dark: fullbright players help the most.

@ assistant ia ai aide help capable peux pouvez
THE ASSISTANT. It explains settings and the game, and proposes changes: nothing changes until the player clicks Apply, and Undo puts the old value back. It can change settings, put actions on keys, open a settings page or the HUD editor. It never runs scripts, quits, connects, chats, records or touches files. It runs on the local translation model.
